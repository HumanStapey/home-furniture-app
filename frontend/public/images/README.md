# Catalogue image

`room-inspiration.webp` is an original AI-generated editorial illustration of a home interior, made with the built-in imagegen tool for this catalogue. It does not depict products offered for sale and uses no reference photographs, brand assets, or production-page screenshots.

The final prompt sent to the built-in tool was:

> Create an original photographic website hero asset for an independent home and living catalogue called Home Furniture. Landscape composition, approximately 3:2. A practical compact light-filled living room corner in an ordinary welcoming apartment: one simple contemporary deep blue upholstered lounge chair with oak legs on the right, a small vivid sunflower-yellow powder-coated side table on the left with an open book and a clear drinking glass, light oak low shelving, a leafy houseplant and a subtle woven natural rug. White plaster wall, daylight from left, soft realistic shadows, honest tactile everyday materials. Eye-level interior editorial photography, calm balanced composition, attractive and lived-in but uncluttered, no people. The image will be cropped horizontally as the right half of a catalogue banner; keep the chair and yellow table together in the central area with comfortable safe space around them. The style should feel practical, inclusive and attainable. All furniture must be generic original designs, no recognizable branded or designer products. No text, labels, prices, logos, watermarks, webpage UI, collage, or mockups. Deliver a single realistic photograph, not an illustration.

The generated PNG was encoded as a 1200 × 800 WebP for delivery. Product photographs in the catalogue remain the images supplied by the existing API.

Manrope is self-hosted separately in `src/app/fonts` under the included SIL Open Font License, downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository.
